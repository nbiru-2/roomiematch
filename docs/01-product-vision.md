# RoomieMatch — Product Vision

## 1. Project Background

Finding a suitable roommate involves more than finding an affordable room in the right location. People sharing an apartment also need to consider their daily routines, cleanliness expectations, social habits, communication preferences, and other aspects of shared living.

During the roommate-search process, people often exchange multiple messages and participate in introductory calls or viewings to determine whether they would be comfortable living together.

However, important differences in expectations may only become apparent after considerable time has already been invested.

RoomieMatch was inspired by firsthand experience navigating shared apartment searches in Germany, particularly through platforms such as WG-Gesucht.

## 2. Problem Statement

People searching for roommates and those offering rooms in shared apartments often lack a structured way to evaluate living compatibility early in the application process.

Housing listings typically emphasize practical information such as rent, location, room size, and availability. Information about household expectations and individual living preferences may be incomplete or scattered across descriptions and conversations.

As a result, both applicants and existing roommates may spend considerable time communicating before discovering significant differences in their expectations.

The problem RoomieMatch aims to address is the difficulty of identifying potential roommate compatibility efficiently, transparently, and early in the search process.

## 3. Proposed Solution

RoomieMatch is a web-based, two-way roommate compatibility assessment platform designed to help individuals and shared households evaluate their living preferences before committing to introductory meetings or further discussions.

Existing roommates can create a household profile, define their living expectations, and generate a shareable questionnaire for prospective roommates.

Applicants complete the questionnaire by providing information about their own habits, preferences, and expectations.

The platform compares both parties' responses and generates a compatibility report highlighting shared preferences, potential differences, and topics that may require further discussion.

Both parties have access to their relevant compatibility results. Existing roommates can review and compare responses from multiple applicants, while individual applicants can view their own compatibility with the household without accessing other applicants' information.

The platform aims to make roommate searches more transparent, efficient, and mutually beneficial.

RoomieMatch is intended to complement existing housing platforms, such as WG-Gesucht, rather than replace them.

## 4. Target Users

RoomieMatch is initially designed for individuals living in or searching for shared apartments (WGs) in Germany. The platform focuses on people who want to understand potential roommate compatibility before investing significant time in conversations, introductory calls, or apartment viewings.

The application serves two primary user groups: existing households looking for a new roommate and individuals searching for a room.

### 4.1 Existing Households (WG Hosts)

This group includes individuals or groups of roommates who have an available room and are searching for someone to join their household.

WG hosts may receive numerous applications from interested individuals. Although housing applications often contain basic personal information, they may not provide enough detail about applicants' daily habits, expectations, and preferred living arrangements.

As a result, hosts may need to exchange repetitive messages or organize multiple introductory conversations before identifying potential compatibility issues.

**Primary Needs**

- Communicate household expectations clearly to potential roommates.
- Understand applicants' lifestyles and living preferences.
- Compare compatibility with multiple applicants in a structured way.
- Identify important similarities and differences before arranging meetings.
- Reduce repetitive communication during the initial roommate-selection process.

**How RoomieMatch Helps**

RoomieMatch allows hosts to create a household profile, establish living preferences, and share a questionnaire with multiple applicants.

Hosts can review individual compatibility reports and compare applicants based on their responses. The results highlight areas of agreement and topics that may require further discussion.

The platform supports the decision-making process without automatically deciding which applicant should be selected.

### 4.2 Room Applicants

This group includes individuals who are searching for a room in a shared apartment and want to find a household that suits their lifestyle, preferences, and expectations.

Applicants may contact numerous WGs before finding a suitable arrangement. Important details about household routines, cleanliness expectations, language preferences, social habits, and guest policies may not always be included in apartment listings.

Consequently, applicants may invest time in conversations and viewings only to discover that the household is not compatible with their expectations.

**Primary Needs**

- Understand household expectations before committing to introductory meetings.
- Communicate personal living habits and preferences clearly.
- Identify similarities and differences between their preferences and those of a potential WG.
- Evaluate whether a shared apartment would suit their lifestyle.
- Make informed decisions about which opportunities to pursue.

**How RoomieMatch Helps**

Applicants can access a shared questionnaire link and provide information about their living habits and expectations.

After completing the questionnaire, applicants can view their own compatibility results with the household.

The report explains shared preferences, potential disagreements, and important topics to discuss.

Applicants do not have access to other applicants' personal information or compatibility reports.

### 4.3 Initial Target Market

The initial geographic focus will be Germany, where shared apartments are commonly known as Wohngemeinschaften (WGs).

The platform will initially focus on students, young professionals, and other adults seeking shared accommodation.

RoomieMatch will be designed to support people with different cultural backgrounds, languages, and living preferences.

The first release will prioritize a straightforward questionnaire-sharing experience rather than building a complete housing marketplace.

### 4.4 Product Principle: Mutual Compatibility

RoomieMatch is built around the principle that roommate compatibility should be evaluated from both perspectives.

Neither the host nor the applicant should be treated as the sole decision-maker in determining whether a living arrangement is suitable.

Both parties should have access to understandable information about their compatibility, while maintaining appropriate control over their personal information.

Compatibility results are intended to support conversations and informed decisions rather than guarantee successful living arrangements.
## 5. Product Goals and Success Metrics

### 5.1 Product Goals

The primary goal of RoomieMatch is to help individuals and shared households identify potential living compatibility earlier in the roommate-search process.

The platform aims to reduce unnecessary introductory conversations, improve transparency about living expectations, and support informed decisions for both applicants and existing roommates.

**Goal 1: Simplify the Initial Roommate Evaluation Process**

RoomieMatch aims to provide a structured way for people to communicate their living preferences before arranging introductory meetings.

By collecting relevant information through a shared questionnaire, the platform seeks to reduce repetitive questions and unnecessary back-and-forth communication.

**Goal 2: Promote Mutual Understanding**

The application aims to help both existing roommates and prospective applicants understand each other's living habits, expectations, and preferences.

Rather than presenting compatibility as a one-sided assessment, RoomieMatch will provide both parties with relevant feedback about their alignment.

**Goal 3: Improve Transparency in Roommate Searches**

RoomieMatch aims to make important household expectations more visible earlier in the search process.

Users should be able to identify potential disagreements involving cleanliness, social habits, daily routines, guests, and communication preferences before committing additional time to the process.

**Goal 4: Support Better-Informed Decisions**

The application will provide understandable compatibility explanations rather than relying exclusively on numerical scores.

These explanations should help users identify important discussion topics and evaluate whether a living arrangement may suit their expectations.

RoomieMatch will not automatically determine whether two parties should live together.

**Goal 5: Deliver an Accessible and Practical Solution**

The first version of RoomieMatch will be a responsive web application accessible through a shareable link.

The applicant experience should be simple and should not require downloading a mobile application.

The platform will prioritize clear navigation, understandable questions, and a straightforward results presentation.

### 5.2 Success Metrics

The success of RoomieMatch will be evaluated through a combination of product usage, task completion, and qualitative user feedback.

The initial metrics will focus on whether users can complete the intended workflows and whether the resulting compatibility information is useful.

**Metric 1: Questionnaire Completion Rate**

This metric measures the percentage of applicants who begin a questionnaire and successfully submit their responses.

A high completion rate may indicate that the questionnaire is understandable and manageable in length.

Calculation:

Questionnaire Completion Rate = (Completed Questionnaires / Started Questionnaires) × 100

**Metric 2: Questionnaire Completion Time**

This metric measures how long applicants take to complete a compatibility questionnaire.

The initial design target is approximately three to five minutes, subject to validation through usability testing.

The goal is to collect meaningful information without making the application process unnecessarily lengthy.

**Metric 3: Compatibility Report Usefulness**

After viewing their results, users may be asked whether the compatibility report helped them understand the household or applicant better.

Feedback will assess whether the results are clear, relevant, and useful for identifying potential disagreements.

An initial research target is for at least 80% of a small pilot group to rate the report as useful. This is a proposed target rather than an established benchmark.

**Metric 4: Early Identification of Compatibility Differences**

This metric evaluates whether RoomieMatch helps users recognize important differences before arranging an introductory conversation or viewing.

Participants will be asked whether the questionnaire revealed something they would otherwise have discovered later in the roommate-search process.

**Metric 5: Successful End-to-End Usage**

This metric measures whether a household can successfully create a questionnaire, share the invitation, receive responses from multiple applicants, and review compatibility results.

The corresponding applicant journey must also work, including accessing the questionnaire, submitting answers, and viewing personal compatibility feedback.

### 5.3 Initial Validation Approach

The initial product will be evaluated through usability testing with a small group of people who have experience searching for or offering rooms in shared apartments.

Testing will focus on task completion, questionnaire clarity, the usefulness of compatibility explanations, and whether the experience supports decisions for both parties.

Research findings and observed usage patterns will be used to refine the success criteria before and after the first release.

### 5.4 Long-Term Success

Long-term success will not be measured solely by the number of registered users or completed questionnaires.

The broader objective is to determine whether RoomieMatch meaningfully improves the roommate-search experience by reducing unnecessary communication, increasing transparency, and helping users make more informed decisions.

The product will be considered valuable if users find the compatibility information useful enough to incorporate it into their actual roommate-search process.
## 6. Product Scope, Assumptions, and Constraints

### 6.1 Initial Product Scope

The initial scope of RoomieMatch focuses on providing a structured, two-way compatibility assessment between existing shared households and prospective roommates.

The platform will allow existing roommates to create a household profile, define their living expectations, and generate a shareable questionnaire that can be distributed to multiple applicants.

Applicants will complete the questionnaire by describing their own living habits, preferences, and expectations. The application will compare these responses against the household's preferences and generate personalized compatibility results.

Both parties will benefit from the assessment. Hosts will be able to review individual responses and compare applicants, while applicants will be able to view their own compatibility with the household.

The compatibility reports will highlight areas of agreement, potential differences, and topics that may require further discussion.

The initial product will focus on supporting the early stages of roommate selection rather than managing the entire housing application process.

### 6.2 Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The Minimum Viable Product will be the simplest functional version of RoomieMatch that allows users to complete the core compatibility assessment process.

The proposed MVP will support household profile creation, preference questionnaires, shareable invitation links, applicant submissions, compatibility calculations, and a dashboard for reviewing results.

A household representative will be able to create and manage a questionnaire, while multiple applicants will be able to respond independently using the same invitation link.

Applicants will not initially be required to create an account simply to complete a questionnaire.

The platform will generate compatibility feedback for both parties while ensuring that applicants cannot view other applicants' responses or results.

The compatibility system will prioritize transparency and explainability. Results will be based on clearly defined questionnaire criteria rather than unexplained automated recommendations.

The exact features included in the MVP will be finalized after user research, requirements analysis, and feature prioritization.

### 6.3 Features Outside the Initial Scope

RoomieMatch is not initially intended to function as a complete housing marketplace.

The first version will not include public apartment listings, location-based roommate discovery, swipe-based matching, rental payments, or rental contract management.

In-app messaging, advanced recommendation systems, AI-generated compatibility analysis, and native iOS or Android applications will also be excluded from the initial release.

These features may be reconsidered in future versions if research and user feedback demonstrate sufficient value.

The purpose of limiting the initial scope is to focus development efforts on solving one clearly defined problem and successfully delivering a complete, functional application.

### 6.4 Key Product Assumptions

The RoomieMatch concept is based on several assumptions that will need to be validated through research.

The first assumption is that people searching for roommates experience unnecessary repetition and inefficiency during the initial communication process.

The second assumption is that both applicants and existing roommates would benefit from understanding living expectations before arranging introductory conversations or apartment viewings.

The third assumption is that users would be willing to complete a short compatibility questionnaire if they believed the results would improve their roommate-search experience.

The fourth assumption is that a structured comparison of living preferences can provide useful information without requiring a complex recommendation system.

The fifth assumption is that both parties would value access to compatibility explanations, rather than only the household receiving applicant evaluations.

The final assumption is that RoomieMatch can provide value as a complementary tool to existing housing platforms without requiring users to abandon their current search methods.

These assumptions are preliminary hypotheses rather than validated findings. They will be reviewed during user research and updated based on evidence.

### 6.5 Technical Constraints

RoomieMatch will initially be developed as a responsive web application using TypeScript, React, and Next.js.

The backend will use Supabase and PostgreSQL, with deployment planned through Vercel.

The first version will prioritize commonly supported web browsers and mobile-friendly layouts rather than developing separate native applications.

Technical decisions will favor established technologies, maintainable code, and straightforward architecture.

The application should remain manageable for an individual developer while still demonstrating practical full-stack software engineering skills.

Additional technologies and third-party services will only be introduced when they solve a clearly identified requirement.

### 6.6 Privacy, Fairness, and Security Constraints

Because RoomieMatch processes information about individuals' living preferences and habits, privacy and responsible data handling will be important product requirements.

Users must understand what information they are providing and who can access it.

Applicants will only have access to their own compatibility results and the household information intended for them. Existing roommates will have access to submissions associated with their own household.

The product will need appropriate authentication, authorization, data retention, and deletion mechanisms.

The matching system must also avoid presenting compatibility scores as objective measures of personality or guarantees of successful shared living.

Personal characteristics such as gender, age, nationality, and other potentially sensitive information will require careful consideration. The application should not introduce discriminatory automated ranking criteria without appropriate legal and ethical review.

Real applicant information, private research records, passwords, and API secrets will not be published in the project's public GitHub repository.

### 6.7 Project Constraints

RoomieMatch is being developed as an individual side project with the dual purpose of delivering a usable product and developing practical software engineering and product management skills.

Development resources and time will therefore be limited.

The project will follow a structured development process, with clear requirements, design documentation, and manageable implementation tasks.

The initial release will prioritize functionality, usability, security, and successful deployment over unnecessary feature complexity.

Changes to the planned scope will be evaluated against user needs, technical feasibility, and available development resources.

### 6.8 Future Product Direction

Although the first version will focus on questionnaire-based compatibility assessments, RoomieMatch may eventually expand into a broader roommate discovery and matching platform.

Potential future developments include public roommate profiles, apartment discovery, more personalized questionnaires, collaborative household profiles, communication tools, and mobile applications.

Future features will be considered based on user feedback and evidence collected after the initial release.

The long-term product vision is to make finding compatible roommates a more transparent, informed, and mutually beneficial experience.

However, the immediate priority is to validate the core problem and successfully deliver the first working version of RoomieMatch.
